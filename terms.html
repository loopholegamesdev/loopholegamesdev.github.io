Lassorbit Terms of Use

Last updated: 15 October 2026

These terms cover playing Lassorbit ("the game"), made by Lassorbit Games ("we"), and in particular its multiplayer and chat. By using multiplayer or chat you agree to them.

Playing with others

Lassorbit lets you play with people on the same screen, on the same Wi-Fi network, and online. Online matches connect players directly to each other.

Chat rules

Chat is for talking with the people you're playing with. The following are not allowed, in chat or in player names:

  • harassment, bullying or threats of any kind
  • hate speech, or attacks on anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality or disability
  • sexual content
  • encouraging self-harm or violence
  • spam, advertising, and links of any kind
  • asking for or sharing personal information (real names, addresses, phone numbers, social media accounts), whether yours or someone else's
  • scams, including offers of free coins, items or accounts
  • pretending to be Lassorbit Games or a member of our team

Chat is filtered automatically for offensive words and links, but the filter can't catch everything.

Blocking and reporting

Open PLAYERS in the chat to block a player (you will no longer see their messages) or report them. A report opens an email to us with their player name and recent messages. We review reports and act on them, which can include removing a player's access to online play.

Fair play

Don't cheat: no modified game files, tools that change the game, or exploits that give an unfair advantage or spoil other players' matches. The game checks matches for impossible moves and removes players whose game doesn't match everyone else's.

Purchases and ads

The Supporter Pack is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play under Google's terms. It changes cosmetics and turns off ads; it doesn't affect gameplay. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Availability

Online play depends on players being able to connect to each other and on third-party services. We can't guarantee it will always be available, and we may change or end online features.
