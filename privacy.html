Loophole Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Loophole ("the game") is made by Loophole Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account, and we don't run servers that collect your data.
  • Your progress (scores, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The mobile version shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a bonus. Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases (the Supporter Pack) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings, unlocked items, any redeemed codes and a history of your recent runs (scores, builds and how each run went, for your own stats) locally on your device. The run history leaves your device only if you choose to share it from the Stats screen; otherwise this data never leaves your device, and we can't access it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (mobile version)

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time under Supporter Pack > Ad privacy options.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Buying the Supporter Pack turns ads off completely.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can unlock it.

The PC version

The PC version has no ads and collects no data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding online play), we will update this policy and the date above.
