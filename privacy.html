Lassorbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Lassorbit ("the game") is made by Lassorbit Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • In multiplayer, your game talks directly to the other players' games. They receive your player name, your moves and your chat messages, and their devices can see your IP address, as with any direct connection.
  • To help players find each other online, the game lists open lobbies on a matchmaking service. A listing is removed when the lobby closes.
  • We don't store chat messages, and we don't keep logs of who played with whom.
  • The mobile version shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one. Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases (Glimmer packs and the Supporter Pack) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings, unlocked items, redeemed codes, the players you have blocked, and a history of your recent runs (scores, builds and how each run went) on your device. The run history leaves your device only if you choose to share it from the Stats screen. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this.

Multiplayer

Local play (same screen, or the same Wi-Fi network) never leaves your home network.

Online play is peer-to-peer: the players in a match connect directly to each other. During a match, the following is exchanged with the other players' devices:

  • your player name, chosen character and cosmetic choices
  • your in-game inputs (steering, upgrade choices, abilities)
  • chat messages you send
  • your IP address and network port, which every direct connection reveals to the other side

The game signs in to the matchmaking service anonymously: it gets a random ID, with no name, email or other personal details attached. To find matches, the game uses a matchmaking listing hosted on Google Firebase (provided by Google LLC). While you host or look for a lobby, the listing holds the lobby's name, player count, game mode, a rough region (such as "Oceania", taken from your device's time zone) and the network address other players need to connect. Listings are deleted when the lobby closes and expire automatically after an hour. Google processes this data on our behalf; see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

To work out how to connect through home routers, the game contacts Google's public STUN servers, which see your IP address only to tell the game what it looks like from the internet.

Chat messages are filtered on every device for offensive words and links. Messages are sent only to the players in your match and aren't stored by us.

Reporting. If you report a player, your email app opens a message to us containing their player name and their recent chat messages. We use reports only to deal with abuse, and delete them once dealt with.

Fair play. To keep matches fair, every player's game checks that the others' moves are possible and that everyone's game stays in sync. This happens during the match and nothing is stored.

Advertising (mobile version)

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time under Shop > Supporter Pack > Ad privacy options.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Buying the Supporter Pack turns ads off completely.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can unlock it.

The PC version

The PC version has no ads. Multiplayer on PC works as described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device: uninstalling deletes it. Lobby listings delete themselves. To ask about a report or anything else, email us.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and the date above.
